No sample/mock prices or amortization numbers may be presented as verified real-world data.

Use external APIs only where explicitly documented. If blocked by missing credentials or human-operated vendor setup, leave a clear BLOCKED entry in PROGRESS.md and continue independent tasks. Do not say 'done' until tests pass. When all tasks are complete, provide launch checklist and report URLs only if actually deployed.
